Volume is the wrong thing to pay for. The easiest volume to pull is on the smallest caps, and the people who bought never show up in the math.

CALLED pays on results instead.

→ Every call gets a receipt. The price is stamped the second the caller signs, and priced again 24 hours later
→ Every wallet that bought after the call is read from the chain. Their median result at +24h is the score
→ The caller's own wallet is watched for the whole window. Sell into your call and it prints zero
→ Half of the 1% platform fee on every trade here fills a weekly pot. The top 5 callers split it

The score in one line:
followers' median at +24h × how many real buyers came in.

Buys under 0.05 SOL don't count. A gain counts in full at 40 real buyers. A loss always counts.

Only callers who signed up with their own wallet and X account are ranked. Payouts go out from the treasury each week, by hand, with every transaction listed on the leaderboard.

Launch a coin, or register as a caller and stamp your first call.

## Efter launch 2 – dag 1–2, kvittot (~620)
What a CALLED receipt actually shows, line by line.

At the call: the price, stamped the moment the caller signs.

+24h: the price again.

Real buyers after: every wallet whose first buy came after the call, 0.05 SOL or more, read from the chain. The caller and the coin's dev don't count.

Followers made: the median result of those buyers at +24h.

Caller sold in window: yes or no, from the wallet the caller registered with.

Points: what the followers made, weighted by how many of them there were.

Every call gets its own page you can share. Open the leaderboard and click any receipt.
